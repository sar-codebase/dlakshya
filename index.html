      Support & Clarity

      Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

      Click any question below to expand and view the answer.

      Q: Is D-Lakshya a one-time assessment test, or can it be used repeatedly?
      A: D-Lakshya is designed for continuous, repetitive assessments throughout the academic year. Teachers can upload new chapter question banks or adjust tests based on current classroom progress. Students can retake assessments multiple times to track growth, master weak areas, and view long-term performance trendlines.
      Q: Do I need an active internet connection to run tests?
      A: No! D-Lakshya functions as an Offline-First Browser Application. Once loaded into your browser (via GitHub, BigRock, or local file), all assessment logic, grading, timers, and reports execute 100% locally on your device with zero live server dependency.
      Q: How do I add my own syllabus or quarterly exam questions?
      A: You can upload your custom chapter question bank formatted as a CSV file directly into the Evaluator Portal for instant local use.
      Q: Can teachers use the generated PDF reports during Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs)?
      A: Yes, absolutely! Teachers can instantly generate and print or save comprehensive PDF executive summary reports right from the Evaluator Portal. These reports include multi-attempt growth trajectories, topic skill strengths, behavioral pacing diagnostics, and custom AI remediation strategies, making them ideal documentation to share and discuss with parents during Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs).
