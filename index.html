    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    Click any question below to expand and view the answer.

    Q: Why are we not able to see or directly open the question bank and student report log files?
    Answer: The question bank files and student report logs are intentionally encrypted and stored in secure, proprietary formats for strict data security, privacy protection, and intellectual property defense. Because D-Lakshya operates on an offline-first architecture, direct external access or manual modification of these raw files is restricted to prevent accidental corruption, data leaks, or unauthorized tampering with evaluation metrics. All parsing, decryption, and processing occur securely inside your browser environment.
    Q: I cannot see or select my student assessment file (.enc) when trying to upload. What should I do?
    Answer: Sometimes, operating systems or browsers automatically append a .txt extension to downloaded files, saving them as *.enc.txt. If your upload dialog window filters strictly for .enc files and hides your file, simply switch the file picker dropdown filter from "Custom Files" to "All Files (*.*)" in your file browser window to locate and select your assessment file smoothly.
    Q: Is my student and assessment data safe since it's processed offline?
    Answer: Yes. All student assessment data, pacing habits, and diagnostic logs remain 100% private and are stored locally on your device's browser storage. No personal student information or performance logs are ever uploaded to external servers or cloud databases.
    Q: What should I do if my student or teacher access key / credentials are not recognized?

    Answer: Invalid access key errors usually happen due to minor typographical errors, accidental trailing spaces, or mismatched casing when entering the Student ID / Client Name or Teacher ID / Access Key.

    Please double-check that your spelling and uppercase/lowercase formatting match your assigned credentials precisely. If your key has expired or you encounter persistent activation errors, verify your internet connection temporarily to re-verify or reach out via our support portal with your registered Name and Email to renew your key.

    Q: Can I use D-Lakshya across multiple different devices or browsers?
    Answer: Because student logs and configurations are securely housed locally within your browser storage to maintain data privacy, data does not automatically sync across different devices. It is recommended to maintain your assessment workflow within your primary authorized browser environment.
    Q: Is D-Lakshya a one-time assessment test, or can it be used repeatedly?
    A: D-Lakshya is designed for continuous, repetitive assessments throughout the academic year. Teachers can upload new chapter question banks or adjust tests based on current classroom progress. Students can retake assessments multiple times to track growth, master weak areas, and view long-term performance trendlines.
    Q: Do I need an active internet connection to run tests?
    A: No! D-Lakshya functions as an Offline-First Browser Application. Once loaded into your browser, all assessment logic, grading, timers, and reports execute 100% locally on your device with zero live server dependency.
    Q: How do I add my own syllabus or quarterly exam questions?
    A: Reach out to the D-Lakshya team via email at dlakshyaofficial@gmail.com and they will assist you with setting up your custom syllabus and question banks.
    Q: Can teachers use the generated PDF reports during Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs)?
    A: Yes, absolutely! Teachers can instantly generate and print or save comprehensive PDF executive summary reports right from the Evaluator Portal. These reports include multi-attempt growth trajectories, topic skill strengths, behavioral pacing diagnostics, and custom AI remediation strategies, making them ideal documentation to share and discuss with parents during Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs).
    Q: For the Individual Plan, who acts as the evaluator or supervisor to review reports?

    A: For home-use plans, a designated family member or parent typically acts as the supervisor using your assigned Teacher ID and Access Key to unlock the evaluator portal and generate local diagnostic reports.

    Note: Upon request, we can also help arrange a qualified mentor or teacher to review the student's reports, subject to availability and nominal, affordable service charges.

    Q: What is the difference between the Personalized AI Remediation Script and the Parent Report Card Summary?

    A: The Personalized AI Remediation Script is designed as an action-oriented study guide for the student, providing a step-by-step corrective practice roadmap (such as reviewing specific chapter notes and doing targeted exercises) to bridge micro-gaps. In contrast, the Parent Report Card Summary is a high-level executive overview for parents during a Parent-Teacher Meeting (PTM), summarizing overall scores, test pacing/temperament, identified gaps, and custom teacher remarks.
